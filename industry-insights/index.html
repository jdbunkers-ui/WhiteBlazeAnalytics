The organizations creating the data share in the value.

Barrel Ledger reviewers, retailers, clubs, and whiskey organizations may voluntarily opt in to commercial insights participation. Anonymous aggregate payouts are weighted toward the organizations whose qualifying tastings are used in the purchased scope.
