01How Revenue Is Shared

The majority of qualifying insights revenue goes back to the organizations creating the data.

There are two distinct revenue paths for participating Barrel Ledger customers. The split depends on whether the buyer purchases anonymous network intelligence or specifically requests one organization’s authorized data.

Anonymous aggregate insights

65% contributor pool · 35% Barrel Ledger

When multiple organizations’ eligible tastings are combined into an anonymous industry-insights product, 65% of eligible net revenue is allocated to a weighted contributor pool. Barrel Ledger retains 35% for the platform, data normalization, anonymization, analysis, sales, administration, and support.

Contributor-specific insights

75% selected organization · 25% Barrel Ledger

When a buyer specifically requests authorized tasting intelligence from one Barrel Ledger organization, that organization receives 75% of eligible net revenue. Barrel Ledger retains 25% for licensing, delivery, administration, and platform services.

A fair weighted model

Volume earns participation. Quality and relevance earn additional weight.

The anonymous contributor pool is not divided equally among every enrolled organization. It is distributed among the organizations whose qualifying records were actually used in the purchased scope.

04Participation & Protection

You retain control over whether and how your organization participates.

Voluntary

Opt in separately

Using Barrel Ledger does not automatically enroll an organization in commercial insights participation. Consent is explicit and can distinguish anonymous aggregation from identified licensing.

Permission-based

Anonymous and identified use remain separate

Anonymous network participation does not automatically authorize the release of your organization’s identity, original prose, quotes, photographs, or other named content.

Independent

Your honest findings remain protected

Payout weighting never rewards favorable scores, positive sentiment, or changing a review to satisfy an industry buyer.
